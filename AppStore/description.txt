Keep your pattern and your progress together.

Row Companion is a focused workspace for knitting and crochet. Start with one of six original, offline draft starter patterns or bring your own PDF. Create a project and track each piece one row at a time. From a scarf's first repeat to a cardigan's second sleeve, see what you've finished and what comes next.

START WITH A PATTERN
Browse six draft starter patterns: three knitting and three crochet, with written instructions, materials, gauge guidance, and generated illustrations. Start Project copies the instructions into editable notes and starts each piece at zero rows. These drafts have not been independently reviewed or test-made; check instructions and your gauge before making. Illustrations are not photos of finished test-made items. Appearance varies with yarn, gauge, and technique.

COUNT WITH CONFIDENCE
Tap Complete row after each finished row. See your total completed rows, your next row within a repeat, and the number of repeats you've finished. Undo a mistaken tap or confirm a count correction when you need to make an adjustment.

KEEP YOUR PATTERN CLOSE
Import a pattern PDF from Files and read it beside your row controls. Zoom in on the details and use the adjustable reading guide to help follow your place.

GIVE EVERY PIECE ITS OWN PLACE
Track fronts, backs, sleeves, and other pieces separately. Each piece keeps its own count, repeat length, notes, and saved pattern reference, so switching between pieces doesn't mean starting over.

MAKE ROOM FOR YOUR NOTES
Keep needle sizes, stitch reminders, shaping details, and other project notes with the piece they belong to. Prefer a text-only project? You can count rows and keep notes without attaching a PDF.

MADE FOR FOCUSED MAKING
Your projects are stored locally on your device. Once your pattern is imported, you can work offline. No account is required. The interface supports Dynamic Type and VoiceOver, with clearly labeled row controls.

Bring your pattern. Find your rhythm. Keep your place.
